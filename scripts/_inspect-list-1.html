Dynamic Frontier Holdings (USA) received approval to acquire Teghout Mining

Dynamic Frontier Holdings (USA), through its subsidiary Cuprar RA, has received approval from the Competition and Consumer Protection Commission of the Republic of Armenia to acquire 100% of the shares of the charter capital of Teghout CJSC.

 

Dynamic Frontier Holdings (USA) has received U.S. Department of the Treasury authorization to acquire 100% of the shares of the charter capital and outstanding debt of Teghout CJSC.

 

Upon completion of the transaction, Teghout CJSC will continue to focus on the efficient and responsible operation of one of Armenia's largest copper and molybdenum deposits.

 

Further information regarding the transaction will be communicated through the Company's official channels as appropriate.

26.04.2025

In accordance with Article 249 of the RA Labour Code and the RA Government Decision No. 1089-N dated July 15, 2004

In accordance with Article 249 of the RA Labour Code and the RA Government Decision No. 1089-N dated July 15, 2004, the health status of employees exposed to harmful and hazardous factors in the production environment must be periodically monitored.


In this context, based on the contract signed between Alaverdi Medical Center CJSC and Teghout CJSC, the company's employees have been undergoing mandatory periodic medical examinations since April 8, 2025.


Teghout CJSC prioritizes the health and well-being of its employees, considering it the foundation of safe and efficient work.
