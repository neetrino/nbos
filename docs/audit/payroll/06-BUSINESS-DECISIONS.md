# Payroll audit — decisions for the Product Owner

**Audit date:** 2026-09-26. These are unanswered policy questions revealed by the documented rules and code. This file does **not** choose salary formulas, bonus percentages, eligibility or clawback amounts. Existing approved delivery v2 decisions in `TODO.md` and `docs/NBOS/03-Business-Logic/11-Delivery-Compensation-Configurator.md` remain in force and should not be reopened as part of this audit.

| ID / priority        | Decision needed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Why it matters and evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Consequence if unresolved                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **BD-01 / High**     | Does a **partially paid Classic invoice** trigger the full one-time Sales bonus on the first confirmed client payment, or only after the invoice becomes fully `PAID`? Specify behavior for multiple invoices/tranches on one order.                                        | Canon says first received money and also describes a paid invoice (`docs/NBOS/03-Business-Logic/03-Bonus-Payroll-Logic.md:25-35,82-85`). Code invokes accrual only on fully `PAID` invoice (`apps/api/src/modules/finance/payments/payments.service.ts:279-294`).                                                                                                                                         | Sales employees may be paid later than intended, or a premature full bonus may be created if implementation is changed without policy.            |
| **BD-02 / Critical** | When a required Sales KPI result, plan or actual is missing, should the bonus be held, paid at full factor, or handled by a documented manual override? Who can override and why?                                                                                           | Current code defaults missing KPI result and incomplete amounts to factor `1` (`apps/api/src/modules/bonus/bonus-payable-snapshot.ts:35-46`; `apps/api/src/modules/payroll-runs/resolve-employee-sales-kpi.ts:61-70`). Canon requires a KPI payout gate (`docs/NBOS/03-Business-Logic/03-Bonus-Payroll-Logic.md:97-111`).                                                                                 | Full payout can occur without evidence that a gate was satisfied.                                                                                 |
| **BD-03 / High**     | What monthly bonus cap applies when fixed salary is zero, and are there role-specific exceptions?                                                                                                                                                                           | `applyPayrollBonusCap` bypasses cap for `baseSalary <= 0` (`apps/api/src/modules/payroll-runs/payroll-bonus-cap.ts:35-37`); canon describes a cap related to Fix (`docs/NBOS/03-Business-Logic/03-Bonus-Payroll-Logic.md:11-19`).                                                                                                                                                                         | A zero-base employee can receive uncapped variable pay, or be blocked if a future correction guesses the wrong rule.                              |
| **BD-04 / Critical** | Define clawback/recovery for client refund or payment deletion: distinguish **unreleased**, **released but unpaid**, **already paid**, partial reversal, and closed payroll. Who approves a correction, and may money be recovered from a later salary?                     | Canon lists clawback (`docs/NBOS/03-Business-Logic/03-Bonus-Payroll-Logic.md:344-365,465-472`); current payment delete leaves Sales accrual unchanged (`apps/api/src/modules/finance/payments/payments.service.ts:298-313`).                                                                                                                                                                              | Bonus ledgers and employee pay can diverge from client cash; an ad hoc deduction risks incorrect pay.                                             |
| **BD-05 / High**     | What constitutes approval of a salary profile, manual bonus, over-funding release and payroll run? Is maker/checker separation required, or may one authorized person propose and approve?                                                                                  | Profile/release APIs accept caller-supplied `approvedById` (`apps/api/src/modules/compensation-profiles/compensation-profiles.controller.ts:40-45`; `apps/api/src/modules/bonus/bonus.controller.ts:173-215`). Canon calls for approval/audit (`docs/NBOS/02-Modules/07-My-Company/07-Compensation-and-Policies.md:88-109`).                                                                              | Recorded approvers may be unauthentic and financial decisions may not meet company policy.                                                        |
| **BD-06 / Critical** | Which currencies are permitted for employee salary and bonus? If a profile is USD/EUR while delivery bonuses are AMD, should payroll block, convert at a recorded rate, or use separate currency lines? Specify authoritative rate/date if conversion is approved.          | Profile has free currency field (`packages/database/prisma/schema/compensation.prisma:77-79`); salary line/expense do not preserve it (`packages/database/prisma/schema/finance.prisma:775-805`; `apps/api/src/modules/payroll-runs/payroll-materialize-expenses.ts:54-86`). V2 canon forbids hidden currency conversion (`docs/NBOS/03-Business-Logic/11-Delivery-Compensation-Configurator.md:98-100`). | A numeric salary may be interpreted in the wrong currency.                                                                                        |
| **BD-07 / High**     | How should salary changes inside a month be prorated, if at all? Which effective date controls a retro correction, and how are already approved/paid months adjusted?                                                                                                       | Profile resolver accepts any interval touching the month and picks latest effective start (`apps/api/src/modules/compensation-profiles/resolve-active-compensation-profile.ts:15-36`), while canon requires historical period correctness and correction (`docs/NBOS/02-Modules/07-My-Company/07-Compensation-and-Policies.md:104-109`).                                                                  | Two salary rates in a month can lead to ambiguous fixed pay; retro changes can silently alter expectations.                                       |
| **BD-08 / Medium**   | What payroll treatment applies to hires, terminations, leave, unpaid absence and rehire during a month? Is the fixed salary prorated, and who supplies attendance facts?                                                                                                    | Seeding includes every employee not currently `TERMINATED`, with full profile/fallback base (`apps/api/src/modules/payroll-runs/payroll-runs.service.ts:158-181`). No proration rule was established in inspected canon or service.                                                                                                                                                                       | Employees may be omitted or paid a full month when employment covered only part of it. This is a decision, not an asserted defect without policy. |
| **BD-09 / Medium**   | For a person in multiple departments or projects, is fixed salary charged centrally, to a primary department, or allocated by a documented rule? Must bonus reporting split by project/order while pay remains one line?                                                    | Membership supports many departments (`packages/database/prisma/schema/rbac.prisma:50-62`); payroll line is unique by run+employee (`packages/database/prisma/schema/finance.prisma:775-805`); report notes department comparisons are future work (`apps/api/src/modules/finance/reports/payroll-report.service.ts:15-20`).                                                                              | Finance cannot reconcile department/project cost reports with one employee payment without a chosen allocation method.                            |
| **BD-10 / Medium**   | Are documented Marketing and Support incentive formulas required for the first real payroll release, or will those departments use approved manual bonus entries temporarily? Name the accountable approver and evidence source.                                            | Marketing formula is in canon (`docs/NBOS/03-Business-Logic/03-Bonus-Payroll-Logic.md:315-341`); current support/marketing templates are manual (`apps/api/src/modules/bonus-policies/bonus-policy-templates.ts:10-13`).                                                                                                                                                                                  | Manual payout may be inconsistent; implementing an unapproved formula would invent policy.                                                        |
| **BD-11 / High**     | What is the authoritative Sales policy date for backdated payments, and what happens if rate rows share `effectiveFrom` or are edited after accrual?                                                                                                                        | Current lookup uses processing time (`apps/api/src/modules/bonus/sales-bonus-accrual.service.ts:196-213`); canon demands a first-payment snapshot (`docs/NBOS/03-Business-Logic/03-Bonus-Payroll-Logic.md:60-65`).                                                                                                                                                                                        | Different operators can obtain different amounts for the same economic event.                                                                     |

How should a partial salary payment be attributed between Fix and bonuses?                                           | Current release is marked `PAID` on full line payout and not reverted when payment is removed (`apps/api/src/modules/payroll-runs/payroll-bonus-release-paid-mark.ts:11-74`; `payroll-salary-line-ledger-sync.ts:51-79`).                                                                                                                                                                                 | Wallet and pool paid totals can misstate what employees received, particularly after partial payout/reversal.                                     |
| **BD-13 / Medium**   | Are tax, statutory deductions, payslips, external bank transfer and employer contribution calculations in scope for NBOS payroll, or is NBOS only a gross-pay approval and payout-recording ledger? If in scope, which jurisdiction(s) and authoritative legal rules apply? | Inspected payroll line is Fix + bonus (`apps/api/src/modules/payroll-runs/payroll-salary-line-total-payable.ts:3-9`); approval creates internal expenses (`payroll-materialize-expenses.ts:49-91`). The audit found no approved requirement defining those additional processes.                                                                                                                          | “Ready for real payroll” cannot be interpreted consistently; no legal or tax formula should be invented.                                          |
| **BD-14 / Medium**   | For a failed Sales accrual after a payment is recorded, should the payment operation fail, or should an idempotent exception queue/reconciliation process retry and alert Finance? Who owns resolution before payroll approval?                                             | `onInvoicePaid` catches/logs errors (`apps/api/src/modules/bonus/sales-bonus-accrual.service.ts:54-66`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Owed Sales bonuses can be missed unless Finance has an explicit control.                                                                          |
