## Other limits identified from implementation

- `CompensationProfile.currency` accepts currencies beyond AMD (`packages/database/prisma/schema/compensation.prisma:77-79`), while `SalaryLine` and expense materialization do not preserve/constrain currency (`packages/database/prisma/schema/finance.prisma:775-805`; `apps/api/src/modules/payroll-runs/payroll-materialize-expenses.ts:54-86`). The v2 canon expressly prohibits hidden currency conversion (`docs/NBOS/03-Business-Logic/11-Delivery-Compensation-Configurator.md:98-100`). This is a **potential wrong-currency payout risk**, needing a staging check and Owner currency policy; do not assume conversion exists.
- A missing Sales KPI result returns payout factor 1 (`apps/api/src/modules/bonus/bonus-payable-snapshot.ts:35-46`); absent plan/actual also returns 1 (`apps/api/src/modules/payroll-runs/resolve-employee-sales-kpi.ts:61-70`). This is a code-confirmed default, while the desired business action is **REQUIREMENTS UNCLEAR**.
- `applyPayrollBonusCap` bypasses cap when base salary is zero (`apps/api/src/modules/payroll-runs/payroll-bonus-cap.ts:35-37`). This is a code-confirmed rule with **unclear policy intent**.
- Payroll report notes explicitly leave department comparisons and scheduled packets for later (`apps/api/src/modules/finance/reports/payroll-report.service.ts:15-20`). Tax calculation, bank disbursement and payslips were not found in the inspected payroll path, but these are **not classified mandatory** without an approved requirement.

## Proposed improvements versus obligations

The defects in [04](04-FUNCTIONAL-DEFECTS.md) follow directly from existing canon and code behavior. The additional controls in [07](07-COMPLETION-ROADMAP.md), such as a locked staging rehearsal, reconciliation report and release gate, are audit recommendations for safely validating real payroll. They are not new approved compensation rules. Product Owner questions are isolated in [06](06-BUSINESS-DECISIONS.md).
