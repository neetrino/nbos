**Current product contract:**

- AMD employee take-home amounts; external accounting/tax calculations and bank transfers. Manual monthly payroll creation. Normally the preceding service month's salary/bonuses are paid in the current month; no hard day 1–15 gate.
- Full fixed salary from day 1 of the explicitly selected current/future start month. Access activation is independent. Month-effective salary changes; no first-hire proration. Final salary/absence changes are entered manually with existing reason/approval controls.
- Sales Probation and active Sales use the same calendar-month KPI model with individually assigned targets. 1,500,000 is an example, not a mandatory target. No automatic target proration. Owner/CEO decides hiring independently of KPI. Explicit Probation with no fixed salary is supported; missing approved terms are not equivalent to intentional zero. Hired low-salary cases use explicitly agreed amounts, not an invented minimum.
- Sales KPI payout factors use the existing scale: >=70% attainment → 1; >=50% and <70% → 0.5; <50% → 0. Missing required facts hold only the affected bonus. No pre-hire Development bonuses and no Sales KPI gate imposed on Development.
- Both Seller and Assistant are required, including the same person in both roles. Combined Sales accrual per order is at most 300,000 AMD, split by actual applicable rate proportions, **before** each employee's KPI payout gate. Subscription first/recurring accrual shares one lifetime order allowance. No employee monthly ceiling tied to salary. No second 300,000 allowance per role.
- Accrual follows full payment of the first qualifying product/order invoice, excluding domain/unrelated-service invoices. Unpaid erroneous duplicates do not reserve eligibility. Block creation/saving of an insufficient first product invoice; its minimum covers the combined capped Sales accrual without forecasting a KPI reduction. No confirmation bypass or cumulative-small-invoice activation.
- Finance/CEO/Owner explicitly selects employee/project amounts, including irregular installments during ongoing hired Development work. No project FIFO or forced equal installments. Actual payment remains distinct from a draft allocation/release. Fixed salary is paid first within a partial combined employee payout. Additional Development awards are separate reasoned bonus entries; original plans remain.
- One authorized Finance/Director/CEO/Owner operator may prepare and approve. Actual authenticated actor, required reasons, correct balances and no duplicates remain mandatory. Marketing/Support bonuses are manual; fixed salary is not split among departments.
- Correct unpaid calculations through controlled preparation; after payment or for closed history use simple reasoned current-period manual adjustments. Keep original accrual/payment history. Returns and unresolved recovery balances are handled manually, never silently deducted from fixed salary or automatically forgiven.
- New rules start in a selected cutover month; Finance reconciles old unpaid balances manually and preserves old payouts. No bulk retroactive repricing or automatic loss of old carry. Failed bonus accrual preserves the client payment and is recovered without duplicates.

#### BR-01 — service month and non-blocking usual payout window

- **Source / related questions:** Owner's first answer and subsequent explicit clarification; Q-01, BD-07 and period terminology.
- **Approved rule:** the current month's ordinary payroll pays the **previous service month's fixed salary and bonuses**, using the fixed-salary terms applicable to that previous month. Keep the service/earned month distinct from actual payment dates and internal payroll labels.
- **Usual schedule, not a hard gate:** days 1–15 of the following month are company practice. Do not prohibit recording or making an otherwise authorized payment before day 1 or after day 15 merely because of that window. This clarification supersedes any interpretation of BR-01 as a mandatory system date restriction. It also applies to the described combined salary-and-bonus payroll cycle; do not impose a competing hard bonus-day cutoff from older text.
- **Illustration:** October fixed salary and October bonuses normally enter the November payout process. A salary change effective November must not change the October fixed salary in that payment. Paying late must not cause the system to select a later salary rate or erase an old bonus entitlement.
- **Payment flexibility:** retain partial, deferred and not-yet-paid amounts; fixed salary is usually paid in full, but that practice must not prohibit other scenarios. The Owner particularly emphasized flexible bonus payout amounts. Choosing how much to pay now is distinct from changing the earned entitlement; no automatic cancellation of an unpaid debt was approved.
